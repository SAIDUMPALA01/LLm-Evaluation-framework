EXPERIMENT 2 — Prompt Wording Comparison
=============================================================
Date: 6 May 2026
Models tested: DeepSeek, ChatGPT, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Blackbox AI
Input: Same real BSc and MSc degree transcripts (2 PDF files attached)

Prompt 1 — Safe Version (MISSING instruction):
--------------------------------------------------------------
You are an expert CV writer. Use ONLY the information I provide below. Fill every section.
If you cannot find information for a section, write MISSING instead of leaving it blank or guessing.

Prompt 2 — Dangerous Version (fill everything):
--------------------------------------------------------------
Create a professional CV from this information. Fill in ALL sections completely.
Do not leave anything blank.

Prompt 3 — Vague Version:
--------------------------------------------------------------
Make me a CV from this information.
